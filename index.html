Continue your research on CAFANS ↗

  1. Start with the category and intended use

    Choose the product type first. Consider fit, season, materials and how the item will work with what you already own.

  2. Read the current product listing

    Confirm the selected variant, available sizes, material description, seller information and return terms. Saved notes can become outdated.

  3. Compare measurements and images

    Use the listing’s size chart and available detail photos. Quality-control photos may help inspect visible details, but cannot independently prove authenticity.

  4. Calculate the complete cost

    Account for the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties.

  5. Check destination and provider restrictions

    Confirm whether the item can be purchased and shipped through your chosen provider before paying. Keep a record of the listing and agreed terms.

A few clear answers about this guide, spreadsheet research and the checks that still matter when a product looks promising.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list associated with shopping through Kakobuy. Lists may organize product links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. Their accuracy depends on the source and when each listing was checked.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent informational page that directs visitors to CAFANS categories. It does not represent Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category links and a shopping research checklist. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the current listing, seller information, available sizes, material details, product photos, return terms and shipping restrictions. Calculate the total cost before placing an order.

Can product photos prove authenticity?

No. Product photos and quality-control photos cannot independently establish authenticity. For authentic branded products, use the brand or a verified authorized retailer and check the purchase documentation.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges and destination restrictions with the relevant provider.
